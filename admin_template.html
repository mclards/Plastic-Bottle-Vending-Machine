Telegram Bot Automated Alerts

How to setup Telegram Alerts

Follow these exact steps to connect Eco-Fi to your Telegram account:

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather. Send it /newbot and follow the prompts to create your bot.
  2. BotFather will give you a Bot Token (e.g., 123456789:ABCdefGh...). Paste it into the Bot Token field below.
  3. Next, search for @userinfobot in Telegram and send it /start. It will reply with your Chat ID (e.g., 123456789). Paste it into the Chat ID field below.
  4. CRITICAL STEP: Telegram blocks bots from messaging users to prevent spam. You MUST search for your new bot's username in Telegram and click Start (or send /start) to authorize it to message you.
  5. Once you have started the chat with your bot, click Save Settings below, and then click Test Message.
